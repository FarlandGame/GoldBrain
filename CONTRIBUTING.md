# Continuing or contributing

Read [the continuation guide](docs/CONTINUING.md),[current status](docs/CURRENT_STATUS.md),[setup](docs/SETUP.md),[verification](docs/VERIFICATION.md) and the published license/asset notices. Fork the source, establish a clean test baseline and describe one focused change with its validation. Preserve both co-creators' credit and identify modifications. The authors make no promise of active maintenance or PR response time. Do not submit private keys,real accounts/databases or unauthorized third-party data/media.
