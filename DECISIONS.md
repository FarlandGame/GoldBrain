## 2026-09-27 — Phase 2 market pulse formula and evidence

- A pulse is a **descriptive 0–100 market reading, not a forecast, accuracy claim, or trade signal**. For each selected 1h (retained from the original chart), 24h, 7d, or 30d series, order positive same-source closes by actual observation time. Require at least four observations, strictly increasing times, no future observation beyond five minutes, no source-stale/disallowed history, and observed span of at least 25% (1h/24h), 50% (7d), or 65% (30d) of the requested window. The newest observation must be at most 15 minutes old for 1h, three hours for domestic 24h, eight hours for other 24h, and four days for 7d/30d. Otherwise display **insufficient/stale evidence and no score**. Closed markets and the Navasan free-tier cadence can legitimately cause this state.
- With log returns `r_i = ln(P_i/P_(i−1))`, sample standard deviation `σ`, `R = ln(P_last/P_first)`, and `V = σ√n` for `n` returns, compute the price component `S_p = 50 + 45·tanh(R/(0.01+V))/(1+5V)`. This centers flat prices at 50 and damps directional momentum in a volatile window. If every genuine OHLC candle has positive volume, compare mean volume in the latter versus earlier half: `S_v = 50 + 50·sign(tanh(R/(0.01+V)))·tanh(ln(mean_later/mean_earlier))`, then use `0.85·S_p + 0.15·S_v`. Observed price samples have no volume and never receive an invented volume component. If a source-labelled GDELT DOC 2.0 news tone `T` is available, apply `0.80·current_score + 0.20·(50 + 5·clamp(T,−10,10))`. If news is down/limited, retain the price/volatility/available-volume score and label the missing news; never substitute a neutral fabricated tone. Round and clamp the final result to 0–100. The score is not probability or model confidence.
- Asset history comes from the existing GoldBrain history API and catalog; Backend OHLC volume now passes through to Flutter without currency conversion because volume is an instrument count/amount, not quote currency. The original visual theme, motion, and chart painter remain. The asset picker is catalog-driven, so assets with too little real history show no score.
- Country index mapping is USA S&P 500 (`^GSPC`), Germany DAX (`^GDAXI`), Japan Nikkei 225 (`^N225`), China SSE Composite (`000001.SS`), France CAC 40 (`^FCHI`), and Iran TEDPIX (TSETMC instrument `32097828799138957`). The five Yahoo Finance public JSON charts were fetched live on 2026-09-27 and are development evidence only; their access and commercial licensing must be rechecked before daily deployment. Yahoo [world indices](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/world-indices/) lists the corresponding markets. TSETMC's [public API host](https://cdn.tsetmc.com/api/Index/GetIndexB2History/32097828799138957) did not respond from this development host; the adapter is fail-closed and its production operation is unverified. Iran 24h cannot be scored from daily closes alone. The undocumented TSETMC field mapping needs a live Iranian-network verification before declaring country coverage complete.
- Financial/macro news tone comes from the [GDELT DOC 2.0 TimelineTone API](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/), averaging its valid time-bin tone values for the selected country/window. This is general news tone, **not** causal market sentiment or a predictive probability. On 2026-09-27 this host saw GDELT HTTP 429 and a connection timeout, so the optional news component was not live-verified end to end. The Backend spaces GDELT calls by at least 5.2 seconds and caches a country/window response for 20 minutes to reduce provider load; the client sees the provider and availability status. No paid service has been purchased.
